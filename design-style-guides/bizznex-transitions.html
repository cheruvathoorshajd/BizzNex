04 · Compare

How the three differ

A · SkylineB · LedgerC · Aurora
Feels likeA brand moment: the business opening its doorsTurning a page in the same bookLight arriving; ambient and soft
Fits Aurora's rulesGood: one accent, the lit windowBest: type, space and hairlines, no boxesGood, but the mesh is meant once per screen
Continuity with the appLow: a separate splashHigh: the app's outline is already thereMedium: opens like the theme reveal
Repeated dailyCan wear thin; best kept to cold startsStays quiet at the tenth sign-inPleasant, slightly heavy on every page load
Perceived waitShort: the drawing occupies the eyeHonest: the line shows progressShort: the bloom is calming
Cost to buildLow: SVG strokes, already the logoLow: text, one line, the layout gridMedium: blur on large layers costs GPU on phones

05 · Your decision

Skyline to come in, Aurora for the rest

Skyline marks the moments someone arrives: the business opening its doors. Aurora carries the quieter moments that follow, so the brand beat isn't spent on a page load. Ledger stays here as a reference only.

MomentDirectionExitWhy
01 Open the appSkylineLiftThe first sight of BizzNex in a session
02 Sign inSkylineLiftArriving in your own workspace
03 Open the demoSkylineLiftA visitor's first look at the product
04 Setup finishedSkylineLiftThe workspace opening for the first time
05 Sign outAuroraFade through paperA quiet goodbye, kept short
06 Session endedAuroraFade through paperExplains the sign-out without alarm
07 Page loadsAuroraFade through paperInside the main column only; the sidebar stays

06 · Rules for any option

No flashOnly show a transition screen if the wait passes about 300 ms; once shown, keep it at least 600 ms so it never blinks.
Page loads stay in placeLoading a page swaps only the main column. The sidebar never disappears mid-session.
Say what is happeningEvery screen names the action ("Signing in", "Opening the demo"). No bare spinners.
Reduced motionWith the OS setting on, every transition is a 120 ms cross-fade with the same words.
Announce itThe status line is an aria-live region, so screen readers hear it.
One scaleDurations multiply by --m / MOTION_SCALE (1.5), like the rest of BizzNex's motion.
