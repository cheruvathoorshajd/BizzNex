BizzNex IdentityProposal · three directions

Brief · October 2026

A mark for a business suite that stays out of your way

BizzNex is calm, editorial software for running a small business. The logo has to feel like the product: warm paper, one colour, nothing shouting. Three directions follow, each developed far enough to judge honestly.

  • Read at 16px in a browser tab, and hold up as a 1024px app icon.
  • Work on warm paper and on night, with the colour switching like the app’s does.
  • Sit beside the Aurora interface without competing with it.
  • Belong to BizzNex alone: an idea, not a generic shape.

The three side by side

Recommendation

Recommendation: The Lift. It is the only direction whose idea comes from BizzNex itself, the floating window. It carries into the wordmark through a single detail, and it keeps the brand’s existing violet and square, so it reads as BizzNex growing up rather than a different company. The Ledger is the strongest runner-up if you want something more traditional and trustworthy.
